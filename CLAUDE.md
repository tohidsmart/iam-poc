# iam-poc

Custom login & consent service for Ory Hydra (interview POC). `spec.md` is the user's original spec and is not edited; `spec.refined.md` is the working spec with decisions and the Definition of done.



## Conventions

- Services depend on ports (interfaces) only; adapters are constructed in `login/src/main.ts` and nowhere else.
- Secrets live in `secrets/` (gitignored, generated by `scripts/setup.sh`) and reach containers as compose secrets. No secret literals in committed files.
- Hydra admin (4445) and Postgres are never published to the host.
